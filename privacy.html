Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 11, 2026

Aidan Beans Clips ("the App") is an internal tool that publishes highlight clips of Twitch streamer Aidan Beans to his official TikTok clips account. This policy explains what data the App handles.

Data we collect

  • TikTok account authorization: when the account owner connects the TikTok clips account, the App receives access and refresh tokens and basic profile information (user.info.basic) from TikTok. These are used only to publish videos to that account.
  • Twitch clips: the App downloads publicly available clips from Aidan Beans' Twitch channel, along with public clip details such as the title and view count.

The App does not collect data from TikTok viewers, other users, or the general public.

How data is used

Data is used only to edit clips and publish them to the connected TikTok account. Clip audio and frames may be processed by AI services (speech-to-text and Anthropic's Claude API) to write captions and choose where to trim each clip.

Storage and sharing

Tokens and working files are stored on the operator's own computer. We do not sell or share data with third parties, except the service providers named above, which are used only to process clips.

Retention and deletion

The account owner can revoke the App's access at any time in TikTok's settings under Security > Manage app permissions. Stored tokens are deleted when access is revoked or when you ask us to delete them.
